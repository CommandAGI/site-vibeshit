How a run runs

Four passes, like a four-ink print. Nothing is made before you approve the proof.

  1. Pick the pieces

    Piece, colourway, where the art goes, how many.

  2. We route the lines

    Each line goes to its own shop with the size run and the art.

  3. Proof in a day

    A proof for every decorated piece. You say yes or you say change it.

  4. Made, then shipped

    Boxes leave the shop. Not a warehouse of ours.

Why the name

The joke is the name. The problem is the pile.

Most swag is ordered in bulk, handed out once and worn for a week. Then a drawer. Then a bin. We named the company after the bin, so nobody has to pretend.

  • Made after you orderNo stock waits for a conference that got cancelled.
  • From 12 piecesA pilot run beats a pallet you regret.
  • Worth wearing twiceReal embroidery, soft enamel, ring-spun cotton.
  • Paper, not plasticPaper wraps and cartons by default.
  • Send it backUnworn pieces go back to the shop for remaking.

We do not claim to be carbon-neutral. We claim to make fewer things nobody wants.

    Questions people actually ask

    Why is it called VibeShit?

    Because that is what most of it becomes. The name keeps us honest: if a piece is not worth wearing twice, we should not make it. The logo is the joke. The production is not.

    What is the smallest order?

    12 pieces on caps, beanies, tees and hoodies; 25 on pins and keyrings; 50 on sticker packs. Each line has its own shop and its own minimum.

    What does it cost?

    We quote each run: quantity, colourways, decoration and the shop’s current rate decide it. Send the sheet and you get one quote with the production window, not a price list that expires.

    Can we see a sample first?

    Yes. A single plain piece from any line ships before a decorated run, and its cost comes off the order if the run goes ahead.

    How long does it take?

    Headwear 14–18 days, trinkets 10–24 days, threads 10–16 days after the proof is approved. A mixed run ships in the longest window, part-shipped from each shop.

    Do you ship outside the United States?

    Yes. Each shop quotes its own freight; the sheet names the destination before we confirm anything.

    Are the pictures on this page real products?

    No. The stickers and the proof are drawings made for this page. Your proof comes from the shop, drawn on your art.
