Why the name

The joke is the name. The problem is the pile.

Most branded merchandise is ordered in bulk, handed out once, and worn for a week. The rest goes to a drawer, then a bin — and the bin is the part the industry does not put on the invoice. We named the company after it so nobody has to pretend otherwise.

We are not claiming to be carbon-neutral. We are claiming to make fewer things nobody wants.

    Questions people actually ask

    Why is it called VibeShit?

    Because that is what most of it becomes. The name keeps us honest: if a piece is not worth wearing twice, we should not make it. We are a real merchandise company — the logo is the joke, the production is not.

    What is the smallest order?

    12 pieces on caps, beanies, tees and hoodies; 25 on pins, keyrings and sticker packs. Each line has its own partner and its own minimum, shown on the card above.

    What does it cost?

    We quote each run: quantity, colourways, decoration and the partner's current rate decide it. Send the sheet and you get one quote with the production window, not a price list that expires.

    Can we see a sample first?

    Yes. A single plain piece from any line ships before a decorated run, and its cost comes off the order if the run goes ahead.

    How long does it take?

    Headwear 14–18 days, trinkets 18–24 days, threads 10–14 days after the proof is approved. A mixed run ships in the longest window, part-shipped from each partner.

    Do you ship outside the United States?

    Yes. Each partner quotes its own freight; the sheet names the destination before we confirm anything.
